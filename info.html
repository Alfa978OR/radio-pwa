Mayelana ne-Alfa Online Radio

Ngubani futsi yini i-Alfa Online Radio?

IRadio Alfa yacala kusakata Tindzaba Letimnandzi ku-FM ngenyanga yaMeyi ngemnyaka wa-2000. I-Alfa Online Radio yacala kusakateka nge-inthanethi ngenyanga yaSepthemba ngemnyaka wa-2023 ngesiSwati nangesiZulu. Balaleli bangalalela tinhlelo nemisebenti ye-Alfa Online Radio ngemakhalekhukhwini abo kanye nangemakhompyutha. I-app lensha, lehlelelwe kahle kusebenta ngemakhalekhukhwini, yethulwa ngenyanga yaBhimbidvwane (January) ngemnyaka wa-2026.

I-Alfa Online Radio isakata kufundvwa kweBhayibheli ngevesi nangevesi, kufundziswa kweBhayibheli, kanye nemicondvo nekukhutsata lokwakha umoya. Luhlelo lwetinhlelo netindzaba letimayelana nekusakata kwabelwana ngato ngeWhatsApp Community Group.

Umsebenti nenhloso ye-Alfa Online Radio uchazwa kahle kakhulu ngalamavesi eBhayibheli lalandzelako: Asitishumayeli tsine, kodvwa sishumayela kutsi Khristu Jesu uyiNkhosi, tsine-ke sitisebenti tenu ngenca yakhe. Ngobe yena lowo Nkulunkulu lowatsi: Akuvele kukhanya ebumnyameni, nguye futsi lokhanyise etinhlitiyweni tetfu, kuze sikwati kubona inkhatimulo yaNkulunkulu ebusweni baKhristu. (2 kubaseKorinthe 4:5–6)

Kungani libito lelitsi "Alfa"?

Libito lelitsi "Alfa" libonakalisa umlayeto weBhayibheli lotsi Nkulunkulu usicalo nesiphetfo, ungumtfombo wekuphila, weliciniso, futsi unguLowo lowenta konkhe kube kusha. Lelibito lisho sisekelo semsebenti webuKhristu we-Alfa Online Radio kanye nekutinikela kwayo ekufundzisiseni iBhayibheli ngekwetsembeka nasekushumayeleni liVangeli.

• "Mine ngingu-Alfa na-Omega," kusho iNkhosi, Nkulunkulu, Somandla lokhona, nalabekhona, nalotako." (Sembulo 1:8)

• Wase utsi kimi: "Sekwentekile. Mine ngingu-Alfa na-Omega, sicalo nesiphetfo. Lowo lowomile ngiyawumnika lilungelo lekunatsa mahhala emtfonjeni wemanti ekuphila. (Sembulo 21:6)

• "Ngingu-Alfa na-Omega, wekucala newekugcina, sicalo nesiphetfo." (Sembulo 22:13)
